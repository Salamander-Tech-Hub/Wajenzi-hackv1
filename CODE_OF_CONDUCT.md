# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Salamander Tech Hub is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

We expect participants to help create a space where people can learn, collaborate, ask questions, give feedback, and contribute openly.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience.
- Assume good intent while staying open to feedback and correction.
- Use inclusive language and welcome different perspectives.
- Give credit to other people's work.
- Focus feedback on ideas, code, and outcomes rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not share someone's personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Plagiarism, impersonation, or misrepresentation.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Enforcement

Community maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other action when behavior violates this code. Decisions will be made with the safety of the community and the context of the situation in mind.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the Salamander Tech Hub maintainers through [hello@salamander.tech](mailto:hello@salamander.tech). Include what happened, where it happened, and any relevant links or screenshots. Please do not investigate or confront the person involved on behalf of the community.

We will handle reports as confidentially as practical, respond as soon as possible, and avoid retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

This code applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, events, chat spaces, and other community spaces operated by Salamander Tech Hub.
